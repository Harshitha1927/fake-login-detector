EDUCATIONAL ANALYSIS

Anatomy of a Fake Login Page Attack

Understanding the technical lifecycle of credential harvesting and social engineering.

01
🎯

Reconnaissance & Cloning

The attacker selects a target brand (e.g. PayPal, Google, Bank) and clones the HTML, CSS, and branding assets to craft an indistinguishable login interface.

Tooling: HTTrack / Scraping
02
🌐

Deceptive Domain Crafting

Attackers register typosquatted domains (paypal-security.xyz) or deploy on compromised IP servers, often abusing subdomains to visually deceive mobile users.

Vector: Typosquatting / IDN
03
✉️

Social Engineering Delivery

Lure messages via phishing email, SMS (smishing), or QR codes are distributed with urgent calls to action ("Account Suspended! Verify Immediately!").

Vector: Phishing / Smishing
04
🎣

Credential Harvesting

The unsuspecting victim submits their username, password, and OTP on the fake form. Backend PHP/Node scripts log credentials to attacker databases.

Action: Keylogging / MITM
05
🚨

Exfiltration & Account Takeover

Credentials are transmitted to Telegram bots or Dark Web marketplaces. The victim is forwarded to the real website, leaving them unaware of compromise.

Impact: Account Takeover

ACADEMIC SPECIFICATIONS

Ethical Hacking Project Overview

Technical framework, detection principles, and defensive objectives.

🔬

Why URL Heuristics?

Traditional signature-based antivirus solutions struggle against ephemeral zero-day phishing sites that exist for only hours. Structural and lexical URL heuristics provide lightweight, zero-latency detection before network traffic reaches unauthorized endpoints.

⚙️

Detection Methodology

The engine evaluates 14 independent threat vectors including hostname entropy, transport encryption, brand trademark divergence, basic auth injection, Punycode homographs, and high-abuse top level domains to compute a consolidated risk index (0-100).

🛡️

Privacy & Credential Safety

This system operates strictly as a defensive URL diagnostic engine. It does NOT prompt for, intercept, store, or transmit user passwords or confidential authentication tokens.
